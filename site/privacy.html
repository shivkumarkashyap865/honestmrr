Privacy Policy

What we collect

HonestMRR is a static informational website. We do not use tracking cookies and do not require accounts. If you submit your startup for listing (via our submission form), we collect only the details you provide: startup name, website, category, description, revenue figures and your contact handle, solely for the purpose of publishing and verifying your listing.

Payments

All payments (UPI, card, PayPal, Wise) are processed by regulated third-party payment providers. HonestMRR never sees or stores card numbers, bank credentials or UPI PINs.

Data published

Revenue figures are published only from public filings/press reports or with the founder's explicit submission. Founders may request correction or removal of their listing at any time by contacting the operator.
