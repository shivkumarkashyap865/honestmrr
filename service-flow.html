💼 Service Business — Poora Flow

6 step. Har step par likha hai KAUN kya karta hai.

STEP 1 — CLIENT
🌍 Koi bhi visitor site par Services page kholta hai (Google se ya X se)
↓
STEP 2 — CLIENT
📩 "Request a free quote" button dabata hai → uska email app khulta hai
↓
STEP 3 — AAP (SHIVKUMAR)
📬 Aapko email aati hai: "mujhe website chahiye, price?" → aap wo email mujhe (chat me) dikhaate ho
↓
STEP 4 — MAIN (AI TEAM)
🤖 Main professional English reply + fixed quote likhta hu, aur jab order pakka ho → poori website/chatbot ka code bhi main hi banata hu
↓
STEP 5 — CLIENT + AAP
💰 Client 50% advance bhejta hai (India: UPI honestmrr@nyes • bahar se: Wise) → aap kaam shuru karte ho
↓
STEP 6 — AAP
🚀 7-14 din me delivery → client baaki 50% bhejta hai → 15 din free support → paisa aapke bank me ✅
Yaad rakhne wali 3 baatein:
1️⃣ Client email se order karta hai — koi cart/button-checkout nahi
2️⃣ Service AAP dete ho — kyunki website/chatbot banana aapka kaam hai
3️⃣ Aap akele nahi — reply, code, invoice, sab me main saath hu
